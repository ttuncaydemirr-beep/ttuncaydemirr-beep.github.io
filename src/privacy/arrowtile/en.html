Arrowtile – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Arrowtile is an arrow puzzle without time pressure that can be played offline. No account is needed; your progress stays on your device. The game shows ads (a one-time “Remove ads” purchase switches off banner and between-level ads) and uses Google services for pseudonymous usage statistics (they do not directly reveal who you are) and crash reports. Below we explain exactly what is processed.

1. Who we are

Arrowtile is developed and published by Demir Games Studio (“we”), an individual developer based in Turkey. For all privacy questions and requests, contact us at nilkaren.tuncay.demir@gmail.com.

This policy explains which personal data is processed when you use the app, why and on what legal basis, who receives it, and what rights you have. It follows the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / UK GDPR) and the Google Play User Data policy.

The app has no accounts, memberships or log-in, and we operate no servers of our own that receive your game or app data. We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts or location. The data described below is processed by Google services built into the app. For the purposes we decide ourselves (statistics, crash reports, ad settings) we are the controller; for Firebase services Google acts as our processor; for ad delivery Google processes data largely under its own responsibility according to its own terms and policies.

2. Data stored only on your device

  • Your level progress, stars and coins
  • Your settings (language, theme, sound, vibration, arrow colour/theme, relaxed mode, aim mode)
  • Daily puzzle streak and trophy calendar
  • The status of the “Remove ads” purchase (cache)
  • Ad frequency counters (for example levels since the last interstitial) and a randomly generated group number on the device for the banner test
  • Your choice in Google’s consent form (UMP), kept in the app’s storage by Google’s SDK

This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings. If Android/Google backup is turned on, this progress and these settings may be included in the device backup in your Google account and restored on a new phone; Google manages that backup and we cannot access it. You can also delete your progress with Settings > Reset progress.

3. Data processed by Google services in the app

3.1 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob:

  • Banner: only at the bottom of the main menu and level-map screens; not during play, at the end of a level, in the shop, in settings or in the daily puzzle.
  • Interstitial (full-screen) ads: at most once every 3 levels and never during a level.
  • Rewarded ads: played only if you choose, in exchange for an in-game benefit (for example hints, coins, an extra life).
  • Remove ads: a one-time purchase switches off banner and interstitial ads.
  • Banner test: to measure whether the banner placements work, a share of players (by default half) is randomly put into a control group that sees no banner; this setting can be changed remotely.

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob/Google may process: the Android advertising ID, IP address (used to infer approximate location), device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version), ad interactions (impressions, clicks, views) and identifiers/storage entries that the ads SDK writes on the device. If you consent, this data may also be used to personalise ads. We do not receive this data and we do not link it to your name or e-mail address.

3.2 Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics)

Events measured: level start, completion and failure (level number, difficulty, stars, mistakes, hints, duration, board size), hint use, daily puzzle completion, ad events (shown, closed, failed to load, delay, error code), banner events, completion of the “Remove ads” purchase, some setting changes (relaxed mode, aim mode, appearance, arrow theme), screen layout problems and technical events such as audio-system recovery. The only user properties written are the app language and the banner group (banner / control). The purposes are to tune difficulty, find bugs, measure ad placements and improve stability.

Google Firebase Analytics collects pseudonymous usage events so that we can understand how the app is used. In addition, Firebase automatically processes information such as an app instance ID (a random installation ID), device model, OS version, app version, language and approximate location derived from the IP address (country/city level). Google signals and the use of Analytics data for ad personalisation are switched off in the app; the data is not linked to your name, e-mail or any free text you type. In the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK and Switzerland, Analytics does not run unless you give consent.

3.3 Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

When the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report: device model, OS version, stack trace, the state of the app at the time of the crash and a random installation ID. Crash reports are sent to keep the app stable and secure and (unlike Analytics) do not depend on the consent form; your right to object to this processing remains (see section 9).

3.4 Remote configuration (Firebase Remote Config)

Firebase Remote Config is used to change some app settings (for example which ad placements are switched on) without releasing an update. When downloading the values, Firebase may process the installation ID and technical information such as language, country, time zone, OS and app version. Remote Config and Crashlytics start when the app opens; Remote Config only downloads setting values and contains no personal data. Analytics and ads, by contrast, do not start in regions that require consent until you have answered the form.

3.5 Purchases (Google Play Billing)

“Remove ads” (a one-time, non-consumable product) is bought through Google Play. Your payment details (card, account) never reach us; Google Play only tells the app whether the purchase is valid. The purchase record is kept by Google Play and Google’s terms apply (https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/).

3.6 Google Play Games — leaderboards (upcoming version)

Leaderboards will be added in a later version; current versions do not have this feature. Once released, it works only if you choose to sign in to Google Play Games: Google processes your Play Games profile (player name, avatar, player ID) under its own terms; the game submits your score to show it on the leaderboard and receives the leaderboard entries. Your player name and score may be visible to other players on the leaderboard. The feature needs internet; the game remains playable offline even if you do not sign in. We will update this section with the version in question.

3.7 Inviting friends (upcoming version)

The planned WhatsApp invite merely opens Android’s share sheet with a prepared text. We do not access your contacts, we do not learn whom you send what to, and no data comes to us from this action.

3.8 Diagnostics log

The diagnostics screen in Settings lets you copy or share a technical log that contains no personal data. The log does not leave your device unless you share it.

5. Consent and your ad choices

Consent form (UMP). In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app shows a consent form built with Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). In it you choose whether the advertising ID and similar technologies may be used, whether you want personalised ads, and whether analytics may run. You may decline; depending on Google’s rules you may then see no ads at all, or only non-personalised/limited ads. In regions where consent is not required, the form is not shown.

Changing your choices. In regions where consent is required you can reopen the consent form at any time with Settings > Privacy options and change your choice (the button appears only where required).

Non-personalised ads. You can choose non-personalised ads by declining personalisation in the consent form or by turning off ad personalisation in Android’s ad settings. You may still see ads, but they will not be tailored to your interests.

Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your Android advertising ID in your device settings (usually Settings > Google > Ads; the name may differ by device). You can manage the ad preferences of your Google account at https://myadcenter.google.com/.

You can withdraw your consent at any time; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before.

6. Third parties and recipients

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with other companies except for advertising and analytics as described. The services included in the app and their privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob / Google advertising technologies, Google Play and Google’s general privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy — how Google uses data from sites or apps of its partners: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config): https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

If the law requires, we may have to disclose information to competent authorities.

7. Retention

On-device data: stays on your device until you delete it (reset, clearing app data or uninstalling).

Ad data: the retention period is determined by Google and governed by Google’s policies (see section 6); we set no separate period for this data.

Firebase Analytics: event-level data is configured to be kept for no longer than 14 months (the longest period Firebase allows); aggregated reports may be kept longer. Crashlytics: crash reports are kept by Firebase for about 90 days. Remote Config: used only to download setting values; we do not generate any further personal data from it.

Your e-mails: kept as long as necessary to answer your request and to meet our legal obligations, then deleted.

8. International transfers

Google services may process data in countries outside Turkey, the EEA and the UK (in particular the United States). Google relies on its own contractual safeguards for these transfers (for example the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and EU Standard Contractual Clauses) and describes them in its policies. For KVKK purposes, the consent you give through the consent form or the in-app choices and the applicable conditions for transfers abroad apply. Because we have no servers of our own, we make no separate transfers.

9. Your rights and how to exercise them

Under KVKK Article 11 you have the right to: learn whether your personal data is processed; request information if it has been processed; learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly; know the third parties in Turkey or abroad to whom data is transferred; request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data; request deletion or destruction under KVKK Article 7; request that these actions be notified to third parties to whom data was transferred; object to a result against you arising from analysis exclusively by automated systems; and claim compensation if you suffer damage from unlawful processing.

Under GDPR / UK GDPR (for users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland) you also have the rights of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), objection to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21) and withdrawal of consent at any time (Art. 7(3)).

How to exercise them. Write to nilkaren.tuncay.demir@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days at the latest. Because the app has no accounts, we may not be able to link data to you on our own; in that case we may ask for additional information (for example the Firebase app instance ID) or explain with reasons why this is not possible. Because most of the data is held by Google, resetting your advertising ID, changing your consent in the app’s privacy options and using Google’s own tools is often the quickest route.

Right to complain. You may complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board (kvkk.gov.tr); in the EU/EEA to the supervisory authority of the country where you live, work or where the infringement took place (list: https://edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en); in the UK to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

10. Children

Arrowtile is not directed at children; the target audience on Google Play is 13 and over. We limit ad content to a level suitable for a general audience (PG). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. The age of digital consent for consent-based processing varies by country (for example 16 in Germany); users below that age may only confirm the consent form with permission of a parent or guardian. If you believe data of a child has been processed, write to nilkaren.tuncay.demir@gmail.com and we will delete the data concerned.

11. Security

Because we do not keep personal data on servers of our own, our exposure is limited. Google’s SDKs transmit data over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Apart from backups and updates, we do not access data on your device. No method is perfectly secure; we nevertheless apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data.
